The kit includes all of the materials you will need for lesson delivery **except for the Reflection Booklet**, which you will need to make copies of to hand out to your class.

There are two versions of the Reflection Booklet to help make the lesson content more accessible to your students and provide opportunities for differentiation.

## Standard Booklet

<img src="{{ '/assets/img/rock.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Rock graphic marking the standard booklet" class="float-end ms-3" style="max-width:120px">

The standard booklet is open-ended and is best suited for students who have high language fluency and retain relatively complex instructions well. This version of the booklet has a **rock** graphic in the lower left corner of the cover — a solid and grounded foundation of knowledge.

{% include feature/button.html text="Download the standard booklet (PDF)" link="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-QYfY6ciceMN-lno3ItOrFfp-sC8anD5/view?usp=drive_link" color="primary" %}

## Scaffolded Booklet

<img src="{{ '/assets/img/river.png' | relative_url }}" alt="River graphic marking the scaffolded booklet" class="float-end ms-3" style="max-width:120px">

The scaffolded booklet includes more structure. It is best suited for students who may not be reading or speaking at grade level yet or who would benefit from additional guidance for more complex activity instructions. This version of the booklet has a **river** graphic in the upper left corner of the cover — the river's current provides guidance.
